Domainster

Domainster is an AI-powered domain valuation tool built for buyers, sellers, and anyone sitting on a domain they're not sure about. Before you spend money registering a name — or walk away from one worth buying — Domainster gives you an instant estimate of what it's actually worth. No forms, no waiting, no broker fees. Type any domain and get a valuation in seconds based on length, keywords, extension, and market demand. Built because most people have no idea what their domain names are worth. Now you do.
