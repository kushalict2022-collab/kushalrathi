BiteBalance

BiteBalance is an AI-powered nutrition tracking app built for people who want to eat better without obsessing over spreadsheets. Instead of manually logging every meal, you simply photograph your food and let the AI do the work. It identifies ingredients, estimates macros, and tracks your daily intake in real time. Built for busy people who care about their health but don't have time for complicated apps. BiteBalance makes staying on top of your nutrition feel effortless — because it should be. No databases to scroll through. No guesswork. Just point, shoot, and know.
